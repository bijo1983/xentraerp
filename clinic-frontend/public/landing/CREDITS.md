# Landing page images

All images in this folder are original vector illustrations made for CLINZA (flat, faceless
editorial style in the XentraERP palette). They are not stock photos and need no attribution.

| File | Shows | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| consultation.svg | A doctor talking with a patient across a desk | Hero (large) |
| eye-exam.svg | An optometrist examining a patient at a slit lamp, eye chart on the wall | Hero (small, overlapping) |
| dental.svg | A dentist treating a patient in a dental chair | "Built for every specialty" band |
| pediatrics.svg | A pediatrician listening to a child with a stethoscope | "Built for every specialty" band |
| reception.svg | A receptionist welcoming a patient at the front desk | "Built for every specialty" band |

Stock photography (Unsplash/Pexels) could not be downloaded from the build environment, so these
illustrations were drawn instead. If you swap in photos later, serve them from this folder (the CSP
allows only `img-src 'self' data: blob:`), keep each under ~250 KB, and list source + photographer here.
